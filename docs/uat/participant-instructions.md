# NFA-81 Participant Instructions

## Welcome

Thank you for helping evaluate NewsFlow AI. We are testing the application, not testing you. If a task is confusing, that is useful product feedback.

NewsFlow AI is intended to help a user prepare a reviewed LinkedIn post and an AI-generated illustration from authorised news information.

## Important test conditions

- All news information in this session is synthetic and is not live CNN content.
- Text and image generation responses are controlled test fixtures, not live OpenAI responses.
- Nothing will be published to LinkedIn or anywhere else.
- Generated content requires human review.
- Use only the test credentials provided by the moderator.
- Do not enter real personal, confidential, unpublished, or commercially sensitive information.
- Do not use a password that you use anywhere else.

## During the session

- Attempt each task in the way that feels natural to you.
- You may think aloud and explain what you expect to happen.
- Tell the moderator when wording, controls, feedback, or navigation are unclear.
- Ask for clarification if the task itself is unclear, but the moderator should not tell you which control to select.
- You may stop the session at any time.

## Feedback collected

The project records:

- your participant code;
- broad experience category;
- test device and browser;
- task results;
- anonymised observations and comments; and
- your questionnaire answers.

The project does not need your name, real account credentials, employer, or private content.

## Final reminder

Please judge whether the workflow is understandable and useful. A technically successful page is still a usability problem if you cannot tell what to do or what happened.
